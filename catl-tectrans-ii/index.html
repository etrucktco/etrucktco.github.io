CATL TECTRANS II

CATL sells TECTRANS II on total cost, not on the battery: 12 years and 1.5 million km with 70 % capacity left, 96 % round-trip efficiency, and 0.6 t more payload for the same energy because the pack is lighter. For a fleet the two figures that move the business case are the payload and the 25-minute megawatt charge; the energy price of a swap, and the price of the pack itself, are still unpublished.
