Skyworth Z9

Skyworth's Level 4 heavy tractor, premiered in Hanover: two motors, 610 kW, a 400 kWh LFP battery, and a sensor set built for ports and yards where one operator watches over a fleet. What it costs to run against a diesel is not yet a calculation anyone can make: no price, no range, no charge power has been published.
