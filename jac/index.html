JAC · Truck makers

JAC

JAC Motors (Hefei, founded 1964) showed four vehicles for Europe, all light: the N90 EV and N42 EV electric light trucks, the SUNRAY PRO electric van (European premiere) and the T9 plug-in hybrid pick-up. No heavy electric truck. JAC's European sales and service network covers 13 countries, run from a hub in Italy, and it aims to cover all of Europe by 2028.
