Alpitronic Hypercharger HYC400

alpitronic's all-in-one 400 kW Hypercharger: two CCS plugs share four 100 kW power stacks, up to 98.2 % efficient by alpitronic's figure. The eTRUCKTCO calculators list it as the higher-loss option for an overnight depot. alpitronic is.
