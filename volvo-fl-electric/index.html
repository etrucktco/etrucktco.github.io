Volvo FL Electric

Volvo FL Electric, on show in Hanover: up to 450 km with six battery packs, or about 200 km on the new 14-tonne version with 145 kWh usable. It charges at 43 kW AC (a full charge of four packs takes about 8 hours) or 150 kW DC, so ordinary depot charging is enough for many city rounds. What it costs to run against a diesel FL cannot be worked out yet: Volvo publishes no price and no energy consumption.
