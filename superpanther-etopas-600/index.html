SuperPanther eTopas 600

SuperPanther's eTopas 600, built by Steyr Automotive in Austria and shown at IAA 2026: 621 kWh LFP battery from CATL, 394 kW continuous and 692 kW peak (535 and 941 hp), up to 560 km in real-world tests, 20 to 80 % in about 38 minutes on two CCS2 chargers, 10.8 t empty. About 100 trucks are to reach customers by the end of 2026, per the maker.
