Pixii PowerBase XLP

The cheapest megawatt charger is the one you do not have to dig a new grid connection for. A PowerBase XLP holds just over 2 MWh and gives 1,000 kW back, so the site draws power slowly all day and spends it fast when a truck arrives. Pixii publishes no price, so the saving against a grid reinforcement is the number to ask your installer for.
