BorgWarner LFP Compact Pack

BorgWarner's argument is cost per pack rather than energy per kilo: LFP blade cells bought from FinDreams, one hardware and software base across four pack shapes, 98.5 % of the energy usable and up to fifteen years of life. For a depot fleet that is a cheap, long-lived pack in a shape that fits where a big one does not. No price and no capacity for the new version have been published.
