i-charging All-in-One 500

A 500 kW all-in-one charger from the Portuguese maker i-charging that can charge trucks over CCS at up to 800 A with the optional liquid-cooled cable, sharing its power between two outputs. It has no MCS outlet and no published price, so it cannot yet be costed against a diesel fuel stop; first European deliveries are due at the end of 2026.
