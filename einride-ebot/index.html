Einride eBot

A self-driving electric rigid truck without a cab, in daily service for Lidl in Edermünde since September 2026: up to 15 Euro pallets a run, 25 km/h on a 400 m public road, no one on board. Setting it against a diesel truck is not possible: Einride publishes no battery, range, weight or price, and it sells transport as a service rather than the truck.
