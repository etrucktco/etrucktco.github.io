IVECO S-eWay Rigid

IVECO's electric rigid, 4x2 or 6x2: 280 to 490 kWh (240 to 420 kWh usable), up to 400 km with the largest battery, 350 kW DC charging (20 to 80 % in under 45 minutes with five packs, about 200 km). The battery warranty runs 6 years or 600,000 km, extendable to 9 years or 900,000 km. To set it against a diesel S-Way the price, an energy use figure and weights per version are still missing; IVECO publishes none of them.
