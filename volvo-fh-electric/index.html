Volvo FH Electric

Volvo's second-generation FH Electric, on show in Hanover: up to 460 kWh usable, up to 470 km, and 20 to 80 % in about 65 minutes on a 350 kW charger. It has no megawatt charging and, per electrive, no AC charging any more. Volvo has published neither a price nor an energy consumption, so what it costs to run against a diesel FH cannot be worked out yet.
