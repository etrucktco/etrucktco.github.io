SuperPanther · Truck makers

SuperPanther

SuperPanther, a Chinese electric-truck start-up from 2022 with its European company in Germany. Its truck is the eTopas 600: a 4x2 tractor built by Steyr Automotive in Austria, with a 621 kWh LFP battery, up to 560 km in real traffic, 660 kW charging over two CCS plugs (20–80 % in about 38 minutes), 42 t and EU type approval. In use since July with DHL and others; about 100 trucks by the end of 2026.
