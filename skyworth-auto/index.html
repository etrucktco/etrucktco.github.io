Skyworth Auto

Skyworth Auto, the commercial-vehicle brand of Skywell Group in Nanjing, at IAA Transportation 2026 for the first time, with five electric vehicles, headed by the Z9, a Level 4 self-driving heavy truck in world premiere. More than 150,000 vehicles delivered in over 45 countries; no truck sales in Europe yet.
