DeepWay Star 6x4

DeepWay's heavy electric tractor, shown in Hanover as a 6x4 and a 4x2: 600 kWh LFP, 540 kW, about 420 km on a charge. The only price on record is Thai: 6.55 million baht, about €170,000. Before it can be costed for a European fleet, it still needs European type approval, a European price and a consumption figure.
