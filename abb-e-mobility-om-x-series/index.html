ABB E-mobility OM X-Series

ABB E-mobility's liquid-cooled charging platform for busy depots and truck hubs: 800 kW per cabinet, up to 98 % conversion efficiency, and MCS at 1,200 kW continuous through the OM Ultra dispenser. The efficiency helps the energy bill, but a cost comparison with diesel still lacks the main number: ABB has not published a price for the cabinets or the dispensers.
