MAXUS eDELIVER 420L

MAXUS's first heavy electric truck, the eDELIVER 420L, shown in Hanover as a near-production 42-tonne tractor: a 615 kWh CATL battery built to last up to 1.5 million km, 500 kW, megawatt charging at up to 1,310 kW (20 to 80 % in 25 minutes) and an empty weight of 9.8 tonnes. Maxus quotes up to 550 km, but on the WLTP test for cars and vans and without saying at what load, so it says little about a loaded truck on the motorway. For a cost comparison with a diesel the rest is missing: no energy use per km, no price and no date for sales in Europe.
