Huawei Digital Power · Battery sheets

Huawei FusionCharge DC storage

The figure that matters here is 50 kW. That is all the grid power Huawei says a site needs to grow from four to twelve charging bays, because the battery in between does the work. Round-trip efficiency is given as up to 92.1 %, which is the share of your bought electricity that reaches the truck. Huawei publishes neither the storage capacity nor a price, so both belong in the quotation you ask for.
