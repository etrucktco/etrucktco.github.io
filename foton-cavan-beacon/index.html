Foton CAVAN BEACON

Foton's CAVAN BEACON, shown in Europe for the first time in Hanover: the battery-electric 6x4 is claimed at over 600 km and 115 kWh per 100 km (105 for a 4x2), and charges from 20 to 80 % in 18 minutes at a megawatt charger of up to 1.44 MW. That energy figure is where a running-cost comparison with diesel starts, but Foton does not say at what load or speed it was measured, and the rest is missing: no battery size, no weights or payload, no price and no sales date for this truck in Europe. Foton says it already sells in nearly 20 European countries, with more than 300 service outlets, and plans local assembly by 2028.
