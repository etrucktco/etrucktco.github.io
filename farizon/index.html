Farizon · Truck makers

Farizon

Farizon, Geely's commercial-vehicle brand, at IAA Transportation 2026 for the first time, with the European premiere of the Homtruck, a heavy electric truck with a 600 kWh battery and over 500 km range, plus two electric vans. The Homtruck is not on sale in Europe yet; specifications, approval and launch date will follow. Spare parts come from a warehouse in Gaggenau, Germany.
