Hitachi Energy Grid-eMotion Fleet

A depot system sold together with its grid connection: a railway-style central rectifier (up to 2.25 MW) feeds 500 kW charger cabinets and dispensers of 300 or 500 A per plug, plus roof pantographs of up to 600 A. There is no MCS plug. Hitachi Energy is.
