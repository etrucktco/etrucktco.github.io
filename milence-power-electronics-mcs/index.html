Milence × Power Electronics MCS

The megawatt charger of the truck makers' own charging operator: Power Electronics' MCS dispenser, up to 1,440 kW and 1,500 A, in use at Milence hubs such as Antwerp-Bruges since 2025. At the IAA, Milence runs its megawatt charging demos on Alpitronic's HYC1000 instead, and signed a partnership with Alpitronic.
