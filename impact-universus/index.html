IMPACT UNIVERSUS

The cost argument here is the platform, not the cell: one mechanical and electrical footprint for LFP and NMC means a vehicle maker validates once and can then sell a cheap long-life pack or an expensive light one out of the same hole in the frame. For an operator that should show up later as cheaper replacement packs and a longer parts life. No capacity price, no cycle figure and no warranty have been published.
