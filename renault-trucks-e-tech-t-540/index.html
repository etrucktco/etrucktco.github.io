Renault Trucks E-Tech T 540

Renault Trucks E-Tech T 540, on show in Hanover: up to 450 km and up to 25 tonnes payload, CCS charging at up to 350 kW but no megawatt charging, battery warranty 6 years or 720,000 km as standard. Renault has published no price and no energy consumption, so what it costs to run against a diesel T cannot be worked out yet.
