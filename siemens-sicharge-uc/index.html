Siemens SICHARGE UC

The industrial-grade Siemens charger the eTRUCKTCO calculators carry as the premium, higher-loss option: 150 kW charging centres, up to four combined for 600 kW, with cable dispensers, pantographs or contact hoods. Siemens no longer lists it among its current chargers and its product pages now lead to Heliox; Siemens eMobility shows the newer SICHARGE FLEX in Hall 13.
