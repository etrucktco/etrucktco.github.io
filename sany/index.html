SANY · Truck makers

SANY

SANY Truck (SANY Group, working in Europe with Putzmeister) showed five electric trucks. The lead model, the e263 S / SE636 tractor, has a 636 kWh LFP battery, up to 500 km range, 400 kW CCS charging, 730 kW peak power and EU type approval, and already runs in Germany and Türkiye. Service through about 650 Alltrucks workshops; prices are agreed per customer.
