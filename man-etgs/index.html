MAN eTGS

MAN gives ranges of up to 570 km for the tractor and up to 820–830 km for the rigids, a fleet average of about 93 kWh per 100 km across its electric TGX and TGS customers, and a 20 to 80 % charge in about half an hour on a megawatt charger. What is still missing to set it against a diesel TGS: a price, weights for each version, and a consumption figure for a stated body, load and route.
