Kempower Satellite

The Kempower Power Unit with Satellite dispensers, the low-loss split-DC system the eTRUCKTCO calculators pick by default for fleets up to 80 trucks: up to 400 kW per plug, up to 12 charging points on one Power Unit and 1,200 kW with two Power Units; on the Kempower stand in Hall 13.
