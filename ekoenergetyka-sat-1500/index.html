Ekoenergetyka SAT 1500 MCS

The Polish maker's megawatt satellite, now sold as Axon Sat MCS: up to 1,500 A on an MCS plug, optionally with a 600 A CCS plug, and more than 1 MW. It is in public use at two St1 truck hubs in Norway. We could not find Ekoenergetyka in the IAA 2026 exhibitor list, and no price is published.
