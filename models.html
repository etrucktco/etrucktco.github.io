What this does, exactly

It takes the loaded range and multiplies it by four factors: how full the truck is, how fast you drive, how cold it is, and what kind of road. Then it holds back 20% as an arrival reserve, because nobody plans to arrive empty.

Every one of those multipliers is our estimate, not manufacturer data, and they are deliberately cautious. They live in data/trucks.json under planning_factors if you disagree with them.
