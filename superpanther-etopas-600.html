SuperPanther eTopas 600

A Chinese-designed long-haul tractor built in Austria: SuperPanther has its eTopas 600 assembled by Steyr Automotive in Steyr, on a 621 kWh LFP pack from CATL. It is the truck this site opens on, because it is the one a Belgian fleet can be quoted on today at a price under the European flagships.

2What "range" means here

Two figures, and they point the same way: 500 km is what SuperPanther claims on one charge, and about 600 km is what was driven in testing without emptying the pack. We plan against 420 to 470 km in Belgian conditions, which is the number the business case on this site uses.

Three different numbers

  • 1Claimed. The manufacturer's headline figure. Best case: favourable load, temperature and speed. Do not schedule on this.
  • 2Loaded. At full legal payload on a typical motorway duty in mild weather. The honest starting point.
  • 3Realistic. What a planner should actually schedule against, year-round, including winter, traffic and a safety reserve.

3Charging

The eTopas charges on ordinary CCS2, not on megawatt hardware: two plugs at once give 660 kW and 20 to 80 % in under 40 minutes. That matters for a depot — it is the charger you can already buy.

4Price and running cost

The price is the argument. At an assumed €220k the eTopas undercuts the European flagships by €80k to €100k, which is what the calculators on this site let you test against your own kilometres, your own electricity price and your own tolls.

The prices on this page are estimates. No European list price is published for a fleet specification of this truck. Use them to frame the question, then put the real quote into the full business case, which lets you type your own purchase price.
