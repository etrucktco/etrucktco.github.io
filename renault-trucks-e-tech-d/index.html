Renault Trucks E-Tech D

Renault Trucks E-Tech D, at IAA 2026 in Hanover: the D 14T carries 6.7 tonnes and covers up to 220 km on 176 kWh; the D 16T goes up to 315 km. Both charge at 43 kW AC or up to 150 kW DC, and Renault says AC depot charging needs no heavy grid upgrades. Renault has published no price and no energy consumption, so what they cost to run against a diesel D cannot be worked out yet.
