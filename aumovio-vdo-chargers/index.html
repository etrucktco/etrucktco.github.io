AUMOVIO VDO charging portfolio

The former Continental Automotive business moves into charging with VDO Charge: AC and DC chargers from a 40 kW mobile unit to a 600 kW liquid-cooled depot system that feeds up to 12 charge points at up to 500 A, all linked to the VDO Fleet software. Shown for the first time at IAA 2026. No MCS plug and no prices yet.
