Voltempo Smart Trailer

Voltempo's driverless 15.6 m electric rigid is still a design: the prototype is due in September 2027 and first deliveries are planned for mid-2028. Voltempo claims 5 to 7 tonnes more payload than a tractor-trailer with no driver on board, but with no price, battery size, consumption or charging figure published it cannot yet be costed against a diesel — and at 15.6 m and 42 tonnes it needs a vehicle category that does not exist yet.
