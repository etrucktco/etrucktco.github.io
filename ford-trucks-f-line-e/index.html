Ford Trucks F-LINE E

Ford Trucks' first electric truck, a 19 t 4x2 or 27 t 6x2 rigid for regional distribution with 294 or 392 kWh and up to 250 or 300 km claimed. For a cost comparison with a diesel, Ford gives charging from 20 to 80 % in under 50 minutes at up to 285 kW and a kerb weight of 8.0 t (4x2) or 9.3 t (6x2). What is still missing: a price, a consumption figure, and a battery or vehicle warranty.
