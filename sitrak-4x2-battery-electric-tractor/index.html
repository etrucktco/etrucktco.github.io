SITRAK 4×2 Battery-Electric Tractor

SINOTRUK's battery-electric 4x2 tractor for 44 tonnes, launched in Hanover: 600 kWh, up to 500 km and as little as 1.1 kWh/km claimed, an empty weight of 10.1 tonnes, and 20 to 80 % in about 23 minutes. The consumption figure is a start for a comparison with a diesel, but the rest is missing: no price, no charging power in kW and no delivery date for Europe.
