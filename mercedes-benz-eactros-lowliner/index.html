Mercedes-Benz eActros Lowliner

Mercedes-Benz's electric Lowliner, a 4x2 tractor with a low fifth wheel for mega trailers with up to 3 metres of inside height, shown to the public for the first time in Hanover: 414 or 621 kWh, about 500 km with the larger battery, a payload of about 24 or 21 tonnes. Orders open in the third quarter of 2026 and production starts in the second quarter of 2027. What it costs to run against a diesel Lowliner cannot be worked out yet: Daimler has published no price. And until megawatt charging comes, in the second half of 2027, it charges at 400 kW at most: 10 to 80 % takes about 46 minutes with the small battery and 70 with the large one.
