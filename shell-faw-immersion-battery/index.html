Shell / FAW TRUCKS immersion cooling battery

The figure that touches a fleet's books is the 32 % longer battery life: if it holds, the pack, usually the most expensive part of an electric truck, is replaced later or not at all inside the vehicle's life. The 0.8 % energy saving is small but permanent. Nothing here is for sale yet and nothing is priced, so it cannot go into a business case; treat it as a direction of travel.
