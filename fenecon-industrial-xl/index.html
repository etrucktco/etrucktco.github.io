FENECON Industrial XL

FENECON's own case: a storage system at a charging park in Oldenburg cuts 180 kW off the peak and lifts solar self-consumption to 96 %. The company reckons a large store pays for itself in about four to eight years on energy and peak-load savings alone, and faster when it makes a grid reinforcement unnecessary. The Industrial XL is the 1,500 kW / 4,072 kWh model for commercial-vehicle charging. It can be bought or rented; no price is published.
